```
🤠 Outlaws are raiding the town — and you're the only sheriff in sight!

Sheriff 3D Showdown is a blocky Wild West 3D shooter. Hold off wave after wave of bandits and face a giant boss every 5 waves.

■ 12 giant bosses
Rattlesnake, Mad Miner, Train-robber boss, Raging Bull, Ghost Gunslinger, Outlaw Blackjack, a dive-bombing Giant Eagle, a spike-shooting Cactus Golem, the Twin Outlaws, a Volcano Giant, a Swamp Croc and a traitor Cavalry Captain. In co-op a Giant Outlaw appears whose two weak points must be hit together.
At half health every boss enters 😡 phase 2 and turns the stage against you — rolling boulders, quicksand, tornadoes, falling rocks and cracking ground.

■ 10 stages
Golden desert, red canyon, western town, volcano mine, snowy pass, haunted graveyard, swamp, cavalry fort, a lantern-lit gold mine and a railyard with passing trains. Weather and time of day change too.

■ 13 weapons and upgrades
Revolver, shotgun, rifle, Gatling gun, dynamite, golden revolver, twin pistols, bow, lasso, molotov, scatter cannon, lightning pistol and boomerang. Upgrade them with the wandering merchant and set bear traps and powder kegs.

■ 10 mounts and mount racing
Horse, white horse, bison, stagecoach, camel, mule, ostrich, gold puma, steam bike and mine cart!
Level them up in your garage, paint them, and unlock a unique skill for each mount.
🏇 Mount race: 5 courses, item boxes, Grand Prix cups, a weekly challenge, your own ghost, CPU riders or up to 4 friends.

■ Companions
Adopt a sheriff dog that learns new tricks, plus an eagle or raccoon pal.

■ Lots of modes
🃏 Card adventure: pick 1 of 3 power cards after every wave
🗺 Bounty map: 20 short challenges, collect 60 stars
Classic · Endless mutation · Weekly event · Weekly boss rush (9 bosses) · 🎰 Daily rule
🔔 Quick-draw duels · 🎯 Practice range · special missions

■ Progress and collect
Sheriff levels and titles, 70+ achievements (some hidden), daily challenges, login rewards, season pass, bestiary, cosmetics and photo mode.

■ Play with friends
Co-op, versus, team battles, duels and races (up to 4), a weekly friend league, coin gifts and record comparison

■ Online hall of fame
Compete on leaderboards for every difficulty, events, boss rush, duels and races.

■ Comfortable for everyone
1st/3rd person, auto-fire and aim assist, custom buttons and keys, text size, color-blind mode, sound captions and automatic graphics for low-end phones.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No login.
```
